How this page reads the workbook

The page ships with the Work Plan and Lists sheets from the workbook already loaded. Load workbook reads a fresh .xlsx entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. It looks for the Work Plan sheet, finds the header row containing “Activity ID”, and maps the columns by their headings, so added or reordered columns are handled. The Lists sheet then refreshes every dropdown. Export .xlsx writes the current plan back out as a real workbook with dates as dates, ready to paste into the master file.
